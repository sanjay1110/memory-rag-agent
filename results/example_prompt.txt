## Pinned knowledge
[P1] (migration_guide.md § Step 3: Move secrets to Vault) Copy every credential from the legacy configuration files into Vault and delete them from the old hosts. Reference them from the manifest; never bake them into the image.
[P2] (secrets_auth.md § Secret storage) All secrets live in Vault under the path `secret/nimbus/<team>/<service>`. Secrets are injected into containers at start-up as files under `/run/secrets`, never as plain environment variables and never committed to `.env` files or the repository. The secret scanner blocks any commit that contains a credential pattern.
